# Risk Register

**Review cadence:** weekly risk check; formal review at sprint end  
**Owner:** Project Sponsor

| ID    | Risk                                                        | Likelihood |   Impact | Owner                   | Mitigation / control                                                                                                                                    | Trigger / contingency                                                         | Status |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------: | -------: | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| R-001 | No qualified Human/Fractional Tech Lead                     |       High |     High | Sponsor                 | ADRs, AI review, small vertical slices, independent external gate                                                                                       | If no qualified reviewer exists, real data and production remain blocked      | Open   |
| R-002 | Single-person bus factor                                    |       High |     High | Sponsor                 | Git remote, documented setup/runbooks, small commits, backups                                                                                           | If owner is unavailable, pause changes and recover from documented state      | Open   |
| R-003 | Backend learning creates design or data-integrity defects   |       High |     High | Engineering Coordinator | TDD, integration tests, review agents, avoid broad parallel implementation                                                                              | Stop feature work when invariants or migrations cannot be explained/tested    | Open   |
| R-004 | Self-hosted MacBook/VM is a single point of failure         |       High |     High | Sponsor                 | Staging-only classification, backup/restore practice, no real data                                                                                      | Recreate disposable staging and verify documented recovery                    | Open   |
| R-005 | Zero budget prevents qualified external review              |     Medium |     High | Sponsor                 | Seek qualified pro-bono reviewer early; preserve budget decision gate                                                                                   | Approve review budget or delay real-data/production milestone                 | Open   |
| R-006 | Legal/compliance interpretation is incomplete               |       High |     High | Sponsor                 | Early compliance workstream; paper remains authoritative                                                                                                | Do not replace paper or go live until formal evidence exists                  | Open   |
| R-007 | Patient or clinical-image data is exposed                   |     Medium | Critical | Engineering Coordinator | Fake data only, private storage, RBAC, MFA, audit, security tests                                                                                       | Treat exposure as critical; stop environment and follow incident process      | Open   |
| R-008 | Scope expands beyond MVP                                    |       High |     High | Product Owner           | Explicit non-goals, change classes, backlog prioritization, WIP limit                                                                                   | Defer change or rebaseline scope/schedule through C2 process                  | Open   |
| R-009 | Timeline is based on optimism rather than measured velocity |       High |   Medium | Product Owner           | A0 is a prototype; reforecast after three sprints                                                                                                       | Publish new forecast with assumptions and confidence range                    | Open   |
| R-010 | SME feedback arrives late                                   |     Medium |   Medium | Product Owner           | Schedule 30–60 minute review every sprint                                                                                                               | Keep item out of `Accepted` until affected SME review occurs                  | Open   |

| R-013 | Secrets or real data enter repository/tests                 |     Medium | Critical | Engineering Coordinator | Root ignore rules, fake fixtures, PR checklist; secret scanning is planned in Foundation Task 1 and becomes an active control after its gate passes     | Revoke/rotate secret, purge unsafe data, document incident before continuing  | Open   |
| R-014 | Manual migration introduces incomplete/incorrect records    |     Medium |     High | Product Owner           | Defined migration scope, role verification, completeness review                                                                                         | Keep paper authoritative and correct via audited migration workflow           | Open   |
| R-015 | Private repository plan cannot enforce branch protection    |       High |     High | Product Owner           | Keep Task 1 gate open; require Product Owner decision on GitHub Pro, public visibility, an explicit compensating-control exception, or another provider | Do not claim protected promotion flow until server-side rules are verified    | Open   |

## Risk acceptance rule

The Sponsor may accept schedule, cost, or low-impact delivery risk. Risks that violate mandatory technical, compliance, security, or real-data gates cannot be accepted as a substitute for closing the gate.
